## What is IKWYD?

**I Know What You Did (`$IKWYD`)** is a culture-first meme token deployed on BNB Smart Chain for the entire generation of crypto participants who survived buying tops, selling bottoms, leverage liquidations, rug pulls, and emotionally driven trading decisions.

More than a token, IKWYD represents an immutable truth about blockchain: **every transaction is permanent, every wallet tells a story, and nothing truly disappears from the ledger.**

The project transforms crypto trauma into meme culture and community engagement — a public confession wall for degens, built on-chain.

## Narrative

IKWYD is built on an undeniable property of blockchain technology: **immutability**.

Every trade, every liquidation, every panic sell, every missed 1000x — it's all there. Permanently. The blockchain is the world's most honest confession wall.

IKWYD leans into this with dark humor and community catharsis:

- Buying the top
- Selling the bottom
- Opening leverage the second a position closes
- Getting liquidated in both directions in the same day
- Trusting anonymous "alpha" callers
- Saying "just a dip" from -80%
- Surviving another cycle to do it all again

This is not shame. This is solidarity. Every degen has a story. IKWYD is the token that acknowledges it.
