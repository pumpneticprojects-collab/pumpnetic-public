# Code of Conduct

**Pumpnetic Community — Internet-Native Crypto Ecosystem**

---

## Our Commitment

Pumpnetic is built on the internet, and the internet works best when people engage with honesty, respect, and shared purpose. As contributors, collaborators, and community members, we commit to making participation in the Pumpnetic ecosystem a constructive and professional experience for everyone — regardless of background, experience level, or perspective.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies across all Pumpnetic spaces, including:

- This GitHub organization and all its repositories
- Official community channels (Discord, Telegram, etc.)
- Social media interactions under the Pumpnetic brand
- Any public or private communication conducted on behalf of Pumpnetic

---

## Standards

### ✅ Expected Behaviour

- Communicate with clarity, honesty, and respect
- Provide and receive constructive feedback professionally
- Acknowledge differing opinions and engage with them in good faith
- Focus discussions on ideas, not individuals
- Protect the privacy and personal information of others
- Report issues or concerns through the appropriate channels
- Represent the Pumpnetic ecosystem with integrity

### 🚫 Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks of any kind
- Discriminatory language or conduct based on race, gender, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or any other characteristic
- Publishing others' private information without explicit consent (doxxing)
- Deliberate spreading of misinformation about Pumpnetic, its projects, or its community members
- Coordinated manipulation of markets, votes, or community sentiment
- Spam, unsolicited promotion, or shill activity in community spaces
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you witness or experience behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it by contacting the Pumpnetic team at:

📧 **conduct@pumpnetic.com**
𝕏 **[@pumpnetic](https://x.com/pumpnetic)** (DM for urgent matters)

All reports will be treated confidentially. We will review every report and respond as promptly as possible.

### Consequences

The Pumpnetic team reserves the right to take any action deemed appropriate in response to a violation, including:

| Severity | Response |
|---|---|
| **Minor** | Private written warning with clarification of the violation |
| **Moderate** | Temporary suspension from community spaces |
| **Serious** | Permanent removal from all Pumpnetic community channels and repositories |
| **Critical** | Referral to relevant authorities where applicable |

Decisions made by the Pumpnetic team are final.
